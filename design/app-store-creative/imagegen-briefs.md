# RosterWake App Store artwork

Built-in ImageGen supplied the illustration layers. The finished search artwork includes a crop of the native 0.4.0 interface for each supported language.

## header

Use case: ads-marketing. Make an exquisite, wordless App Store product-page masthead for RosterWake, a rotating-shift alarm app. Wide full-bleed panoramic 21:9 artwork, designed like a finely crafted physical editorial paper-and-enamel diorama. At the EXACT CENTRE, a small beautifully sculpted warm-ivory hanging alarm bell with an unmistakable lip and little clapper, captured a little off plumb as if just sounding. Surround the bell with one beautiful shallow circular dial made of six curved enamel sections in three matching pairs: clear dawn-blue, rich cobalt and soft pearl, representing two early shifts, two late shifts and two rest days. The bell and coloured arc read as a real freestanding sculpture, not an app icon tile or logo pasted on top. Make its silhouette exceptionally legible. Behind and below it, broad, elegant, shallow layers of cut paper roll across the canvas, like the measured bands of time passing, transitioning from midnight ink and cobalt on the left to muted azure and sunrise apricot on the right. A subtle small warm sun disk, a few fine tactile material edges, long delicate shadows, luminous directional side light, exquisite gouache and screen-printed paper grain. Quiet, art-directed, generous, contemporary editorial illustration, limited palette, rich contrast. IMPORTANT: the entire meaningful object is quite SMALL within this very WIDE landscape. Keep the complete central dial and bell within the middle THREE TENTHS of the final width and middle ONE THIRD of the final height. If a 3:2 canvas is used, keep the entire main subject within the middle QUARTER of that canvas height so it survives a centre-cropped 21:9 banner. Use far sides and top and bottom only for broad, abstract light and landscape; avoid an empty featureless background. Perfectly continuous artwork edge-to-edge. No typography, numerals, captions, people, device mockups, interface, calendars with text, logo lockups, cards, border, or watermark. Premium visual simplicity, no busy decorative detail.

## search

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a 3:2 full-bleed landscape backplate for a premium App Store search image about RosterWake, a rotating-shift alarm. It is the second image of a day/night paper-and-enamel editorial campaign. On the LEFT of centre only, a tactile little ivory enamel hanging alarm bell with a clear wide lip and dark clapper, tilted mid-ring and suspended inside a richly coloured open circular dial made of curved paired cobalt, midnight and pearl enamel pieces. Gentle amber glint along one rim. A few broad-cut cobalt and azure paper layers sweep along the bottom like a shift calendar turning from night into day. Deep ink navy to rich muted peacock-blue on the upper half, warm apricot morning light hitting the LEFT object and a few paper edges. Considered cinematic directions, subtly visible fine paper fibres, sculptural edges, delicate contact shadows, confident clean forms, nothing glossy or cartoonish. The sculptural bell and its dial should be centered at exactly 32 percent of the canvas WIDTH and 50 percent of its HEIGHT; constrain the entire sculpture within roughly x=21–42% and y=32–65%. A crop of the central band should still show the bell in full. The RIGHT half (x=45–90%, especially middle) MUST remain unoccupied by objects; make it a simple deep navy/blue atmospheric field with soft light, to receive an exact real app screenshot card during typesetting. Do not draw the card, a device or a placeholder box, do not draw any UI. The paper sweep may continue softly below that area. High-end, contemporary printed editorial art with a quiet atmosphere. Balanced full bleed, not a grid or poster template. No letters, numbers, labels, text, starscape, people, frame, watermark or brand logos.
