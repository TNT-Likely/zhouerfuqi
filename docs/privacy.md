# 周而复起 · 隐私政策

更新日期:2026-11-16

周而复起(以下简称"本应用")是一款本地优先的排班响铃闹钟。我们高度重视你的隐私:

## 我们收集什么

**不收集任何个人数据。** 本应用没有账号系统、没有统计/埋点、没有广告 SDK。
你的排班规则、班组、闹钟设置等全部数据仅保存在你的手机本地,不会上传到任何服务器。

## 联网行为(仅此一项)

本应用仅在启动时访问一次公开的**中国法定节假日数据**(holiday-cn 项目,MIT 开源),
用于更新节假日安排,使闹钟在节假日自动休息、调休日自动补班。该请求不携带任何
你的个人信息或设备标识。你也可以在设置中关闭节假日联动,此时应用完全离线。

## 权限用途

| 权限 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| 通知 / 精确闹钟 | 在你设定的时刻响铃 |
| 全屏意图 | 响铃时展示全屏响铃页(锁屏可见) |
| 开机自启 | 重启后恢复响铃计划 |
| 请求安装包(仅 GitHub Release 通道版本) | 应用内自助更新;Google Play 渠道版本不含此权限 |

## 数据删除

卸载本应用即删除全部本地数据;你也可在「设置 → 备份与导出」中导出或清除。

## 联系方式

问题与反馈:https://github.com/TNT-Likely/zhouerfuqi/issues

# Privacy Policy (English)

Updated: 2026-11-16

**Zhouerfuqi** is a local-first shift-scheduling alarm app.

**We collect no personal data.** No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. All
your schedules and settings stay on your device and never leave it.

**Network use:** on startup the app fetches one public dataset — Chinese statutory
holidays (the open-source holiday-cn project) — so alarms can skip holidays and cover
make-up workdays automatically. No personal information or device identifiers are
sent. You can disable holiday sync in Settings, after which the app is fully offline.

**Permissions:** notifications & exact alarms (ring at your scheduled times),
full-screen intent (show the ringing page on lock screen), boot receiver (restore
schedules after reboot), request-install-packages (self-update in the GitHub Release
distribution only; the Google Play build does not include it).

**Data deletion:** uninstall the app, or use Settings → Backup & Export.

Contact: https://github.com/TNT-Likely/zhouerfuqi/issues
