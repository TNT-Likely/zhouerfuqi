### Name

### Keywords

rotation,night,work,calendar,nurse,pattern,schedule,2 on 2 off,4 on 4 off,shift worker

### Description

RosterWake is a personal alarm clock for work schedules that change. Set a repeating rotation or pick dates on the calendar. Your alarms follow the shifts you set up.

- Start with weekdays, alternating Saturdays, or a rotating schedule such as 2 on, 2 off.
- Set different alarm times for early and late shifts. Add more than one alarm to the same shift.
- See what's coming up on the monthly calendar and the Next Alarm widget.
- Skip one alarm or change the time for a single day. Your regular rotation stays in place.
- Check alarm permissions and schedule a short lock-screen test in Settings.
- Export a schedule as a calendar file or keep a JSON backup you can restore later.

RosterWake uses system alarms on iOS 26 and later when alarm access is allowed. Earlier iOS versions use local notifications. You can see how your next alarm is scheduled in the app.

Your schedules stay on your device. No account is needed.

### Full description

Rotating shifts make it easy to forget which alarm to set. With RosterWake, you set your shift pattern and the alarms for each kind of shift. RosterWake works out which alarm is due next.

• Start with weekdays, alternating Saturdays, or a rotation such as 2 on, 2 off.
• Give early and late shifts their own alarm times, or pick individual dates on the calendar.
• See your next alarm on the home screen widget and review your month in the app.
• Skip one alarm or edit a single day while keeping the rest of your schedule.
• Check alarm permissions and try a short lock-screen test in Settings. Exact alarms and full-screen ringing depend on your device and its permissions.
• Export a schedule as a calendar file or keep a JSON backup you can restore later.

Your schedules stay on your device. No account is needed.
